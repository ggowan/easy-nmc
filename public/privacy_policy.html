Privacy Policy for Easy NMC

Last Updated: October 4, 2025

Introduction

Welcome to Easy NMC. This application was developed for the Greek Orthodox Archdiocese of America to collect financial reports from parishes. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and disclose this information as well as information about you when you use our application.

Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us when you use Easy NMC. This includes:

  • Account Information: To use Easy NMC, you may be asked to authenticate with your Google account. If you do, we collect your Google user ID (UID) to uniquely identify you and secure your access to the application. We do not collect your password.
  • Personal Information: We may ask you to provide your name and contact information, as well as the names and contact information of other people at your parish. We store this information in our database.
  • Financial Data: The primary purpose of the app is to collect financial reports regarding the parishes of the Archdiocese. Any financial data you submit through the application is stored in our database.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Process and store the financial reports submitted by parishes.
  • Support the needs of the Archdiocese, Metropolis and Parishes, including the National Ministries Commitment program.
  • Communicate with you about your submissions or to provide support.
  • Authenticate users and ensure the security of the application.

Data Storage and Security

All data collected by Easy NMC is stored in Google's Firebase Realtime Database. We take reasonable measures to protect the information from loss, theft, misuse, and unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, and destruction. However, Easy NMC is not a highly secure application, and accordingly it is not recommended to store information of a highly confidential or private nature in the application. If you need to communicate such information, it's recommended that you use other means to do so.

Data Sharing

We do not share the information with third parties except in the following circumstances:

  • With the Greek Orthodox Archdiocese of America and its affiliates: The data that's collected will be shared with the Archdiocese Finance Committee, the Metropolis Finance Committees, and the National Ministries Committee. The Archdiocese and Metropolises may choose to share the information further, at their own discretion.
  • For Legal Reasons: We may disclose your information if we believe that disclosure is in accordance with, or required by, any applicable law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request.

Your Rights

You have the right to access the personal information we hold about you and to ask that your personal information be corrected, updated, or deleted. If you would like to exercise this right, please contact us through the contact information below.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. If we make changes, we will notify you by revising the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy and, in some cases, we may provide you with additional notice (such as adding a statement to our homepage or sending you a notification).
